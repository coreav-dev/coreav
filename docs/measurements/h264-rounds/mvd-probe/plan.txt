Cut in view (C, not built):
- the mvd caches packed: one cache a list holding both components' capped absolute values side by side (u16 a block),
  so that store_mvd writes a partition's row of both components with one store of 2, 4 or 8 bytes at a masked index
  (no per-component, per-row loop, no bounds check), fill_mvd_cache starts two caches instead of four with one 8-byte
  load and store for the row above, and the macroblock's edge is kept packed (Edge for the mvd entries as u16 pairs);
- both components of an mvd decoded in one call into the slice's instance (one v3::enter a vector instead of two,
  the neighbours' pair read once), and the prefix (TU, cMax 9) as its own loop: bin 0 at ctxIdxInc from the
  neighbours, bins 1 to 8 at base + min(bin + 2, 6), no Option compare against cMax, no closure, no unary overflow
  check (the prefix is at most 9 bins);
- DecodeDecision, the bypass bins and the Exp-Golomb suffix unchanged; ref_idx unchanged (its context reads two
  neighbours through neighbour_4x4, which is MB-layer work this cut leaves alone).

- The 6 CAVLC streams run none of the pads and are the null control: their median should read 1.000 (within
