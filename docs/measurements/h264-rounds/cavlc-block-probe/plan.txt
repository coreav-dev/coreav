CAVLC residual block parse probe, part-only (written before the run).

Question: does the CAVLC residual block parse (cavlc.rs block_body, residual_block_cavlc() of clause 7.3.5.3.2) cost
time in proportion to the instructions a cut of it would remove? The entropy re-measure on an earlier change's code
(entropy-after-483) puts the CAVLC streams' entropy and MB-layer excess at +657 instructions a macroblock over FFmpeg,
8% to 24% of the instructions of five of the six CAVLC streams, with block_body its largest piece. counts.txt gives
block_body at 0.5% to 26.8% of a CAVLC stream's instructions (5.2% and 9.6% the middle two), 239 to 566 a call.

Cut in view (C, not built), on bundle 2 item A's level reading (perf/h264-bundle2-a a46be406; C leaves levels() and
escaped_level() as item A wrote them, and CoeffScale::apply, which bundle 2 item C changes):
- the code tables of clause 9.2 as statics built at compile time (const fn) instead of a OnceLock of Vecs: no
  initialization check, no Vec pointer and length loads, each code's row width and cap as constants, entries packed
  so one load reads one, indexes masked to a power-of-two table so that no bounds check is left;
- the coeff_token table from nC by a small table instead of a chain of compares;
- run_before from one table indexed by min(zerosLeft, 7) and the next three bits, the run of zeros of the
  zerosLeft > 6 code (runs above 6) from the leading-zero count; once zerosLeft is 0 the remaining levels are placed at
  consecutive scanning positions with no further read or check;
- the placement loop's scan and output indexes masked (scan has 64 entries, the output N, a power of two), so that
  its bounds checks go.
