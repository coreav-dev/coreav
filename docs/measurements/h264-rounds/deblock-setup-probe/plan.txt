Deblocking driver: the per-macroblock setup of the kernels' calls. Probe plan, written before any measurement of this
probe.

Question: what is the most time a change of the deblocking driver's per-macroblock setup could save? The setup in view
is what `deblock_row` does around each kernel call: a luma edge's view (`EdgeMut::new`, one a filtered edge) and its
parameters (`Thresholds::params`: four t'C0 lookups and a 40-byte `EdgeParams`), a macroblock's chroma windows
(`ChromaMbMut::new`, one a plane) and its eight chroma `EdgeParams` (zeroed, then filled per slot). Changes that would
remove it: views checked once a macroblock or once a row instead of once an edge, and parameters handed to the kernels
in the form the tables give (alpha, beta, the t'C0 row and the four bS bytes) for the kernel to spread with one byte
shuffle. Nothing is built for the probe. It bounds only the part such a change removes ("For later probes: what a
doubled call prices"): the bS derivation (`strengths`), the QPs and `Thresholds::new` are kept and not counted.

Build: main at 145232d, `CARGO_PROFILE_DIST_DEBUG=line-tables-only cargo build --locked --profile dist --example
decode -p coreav-h264` (`prof-main`). All 32 training streams at the class frame counts (sd 1800, 720p 900, 1080p 300,
4k 150), `--threads 1 --discard`.

1. Time: perf cpu-clock at 10 kHz, pinned to CPU 2, five runs a stream with `--kernels avx2` (so that the sampled and
   simulated runs execute the same instructions; Valgrind has no AVX-512), and five with the detected kernels
   (AVX-512 on this host; only their total time is used).
2. Instructions and misses: callgrind --dump-instr=yes --cache-sim=yes --D1=49152,12,64, `--kernels avx2`, one run.
3. Per source line of our code (innermost inline frame in a CoreAV crate, the MB layout probe's method): time share,
   instruction share, excess = time share - instruction share, simulated D1 write misses.

The removed set R, by the line of the `deblock_row` frame an instruction is inlined into (deblock.rs at 145232d), with
every inlined callee's instructions (out-of-line instances of `EdgeMut::new`, `ChromaMbMut::new` and
`Thresholds::params` count in full):
  luma windows:   566, 571, 572, 575, 576 (the edge's position and `EdgeMut::new`);
  luma params:    567 (`Thresholds::params`);
  chroma params:  591 (the slots' zeroing), 613 to 615 (`Thresholds::params` per plane into a slot);
  chroma windows: 622, 630 to 632 (the window's position and `ChromaMbMut::new` for both planes).
The kernels' reading of the parameters K (x86.rs and x86/chroma_mb.rs at 145232d, the AVX2 tier the runs use), which
the compact form would replace by a shuffle and is taken as removed in full: the functions `segment_bytes`,
`spread8`, `spread16`, `per_line8`, `per_line16`, `lane_values` (x86.rs) and `lanes`, `filters` (chroma_mb.rs), and
the lines that broadcast `params.alpha` or `params.beta`.

Bound per stream, generous on purpose (the MB layout probe's formula):
  I = instruction share of R and K;
  S = max( sum over R and K of positive excess,
           W x sum over every line with stores of positive excess ),
      W = R's and K's simulated D1 write misses over all simulated D1 write misses;
  bound = I + S, in % of the stream's sampled AVX2 time;
  bound, detected = bound x (AVX2 time / detected-kernel time), the same milliseconds over the faster run the decoder
  takes by default. This is the reading the threshold applies to.

Threshold (an earlier change's, as the MB layout probe applied it to a time bound): build a round on the setup only if, in at
least one class, the median per-stream bound, detected, is 1.5% or more; such a round removes at least the parts
whose medians add up to 1.5% in that class, and its own bars, committed before its run, decide what is reached.
Otherwise the probe is recorded and nothing is built.

Also reported (information): the bound per part and per class, R's and K's instructions a macroblock, and the bound
without the store-excess term.
