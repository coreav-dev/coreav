Reconstruction at one thread: what a change to the coefficient scaling could save. Probe plan, written before the
time measurement of this probe.

Why: the profile at 6c9e240 (../profile-6c9e240/stages.txt) puts reconstruction at 1.32 of FFmpeg's time at 720p and
1.25 at 1080p, the next stage after the MB layer by excess over parity (1.00). Its items (items-rest.txt, means of
FFmpeg's total): the kernels idct4x4_add_quad 0.0127, idct8x8_add 0.0077, intra4x4_mb 0.0077, intra_chroma_mb 0.0069,
then recon.rs's apply<16> 0.0069, prepare_level_scales 0.0045, reconstruct_chroma 0.0032, apply<64> 0.0029. The
coreav-h264 crate is unchanged from 6c9e240 to main 4f456984 (only the kernels crate changed since).

The change in view (not built for the probe): coefficient scaling from per-QP tables with a fixed shift.
  - A slice keeps, per scaling slot (luma 4x4 intra and inter, luma 8x8 intra and inter, chroma Cb and Cr intra and
    inter), the CoeffScale of each QP it has used, built on first use, so a macroblock whose QP differs from the one
    before looks its scalings up instead of rebuilding them (CoeffScale::new copies and shifts 16 or 64 factors).
  - QP'C (chroma_qp, clause 8.5.8) comes with the chroma entry, not from a lookup per macroblock and component.
  - Every scaling takes the form (level * (LevelScale << (qP / 6)) + 2^(r-1)) >> r with r = 4 for 4x4 and 6 for 8x8
    blocks: for qP / 6 < r this equals the clause's (level * LevelScale + 2^(r-qP/6-1)) >> (r - qP/6) (numerator and
    denominator multiplied by 2^(qP/6)), and for qP / 6 >= r it equals (level * LevelScale) << (qP/6 - r) (the added
    2^(r-1) is below 2^r and leaves the floor unchanged). The round and the shift become constants in apply.
What it removes: CoeffScale::new, prepare_level_scales except one look-up a slot, chroma_qp, and part of apply.

Build: main at 4f456984, `CARGO_PROFILE_DIST_DEBUG=line-tables-only cargo build --locked --profile dist --example
decode -p coreav-h264`. Both measurements run `--kernels avx2 --threads 1 --discard` so that the sampled and the
simulated runs execute the same instructions (Valgrind has no AVX-512), at the class frame counts (sd 1800, 720p 900,
1080p 300, 4k 150), all 32 training streams; the method of ../mb-layout-probe/ (perf.sh, cg.sh without the cache
simulation, the per-line join of mbs.py).
1. Time: perf cpu-clock at 10 kHz, pinned to CPU 2, five runs a stream, nothing else running.
2. Instructions: callgrind --dump-instr=yes, one run.
3. Per source line of our code (innermost inline frame in a CoreAV crate): time share t and instruction share i of
   the stream.

The removed set R, by innermost frame in recon.rs: CoeffScale::new, CoeffScale::apply (wherever inlined: the CABAC and
CAVLC level loops), prepare_level_scales, chroma_qp. All of R's lines are taken as removed, apply's included, though
the change keeps apply's multiplication and clip.

Bound per stream, in % of its sampled time, generous on purpose: bound = sum over R's lines of max(t, i) (each line's
instructions and any time sampled above them, skid included, taken as removed).

Threshold (an earlier change's, as ../mb-layout-probe/plan.txt applies it to a time bound): build the change only if, in at least
one class, the median per-stream bound is 1.5% or more. Otherwise the probe is recorded and nothing is built.

Reported as information, not judged: per class, the medians of max(t, i) summed over the rest of recon.rs's lines
(the reconstruction glue: reconstruct_intra, reconstruct_inter, reconstruct_chroma, residual_4x4_luma, chroma_dc,
luma_dc, the offsets), and of t and i for the intra 4x4 kernel (every line whose inline chain includes intra4x4_mb),
the plan's other named item. An exploratory callgrind of main (one thread, --discard, callgrind's class counts 240,
120, 60 and 30, default kernels), taken before this plan, gave R's instruction share as class medians 0.49% (sd),
0.38% (720p), 0.54% (1080p) and 0.30% (4k), and the whole of recon.rs's lines 1.24%, 1.70%, 1.75% and 1.15%; it is
kept as cg-exploratory.txt and plays no part in the judgment.
